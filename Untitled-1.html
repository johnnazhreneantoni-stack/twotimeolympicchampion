Welcome to League of Legends

League of Legends (LoL), commonly referred to as League, is a multiplayer online battle arena video game developed and published by Riot Games.
Inspired by Defense of the Ancients, a custom map for Warcraft III, Riot's founders sought to develop a stand-alone game in the same genre.
Since its release in October 2009, League has been free-to-play and is monetized through purchasable character customization.
The game is available for Windows and macOS. In the game's main mode, Summoner's Rift, two teams of five players battle in player-versus-player combat.
Each player controls a character, known as a "champion", with unique abilities and styles of play.
During a match, champions become more powerful by collecting experience points, earning gold, and purchasing items to defeat the opposing team.
Teams defend their base and win by pushing toward the enemy base and destroying a large structure within it, the "Nexus".

Skills

League of Legends skills involve mastering a champion's unique kit—typically four abilities (Q, W, E, R) and a passive—along with two customizable Summoner Spells mapped to D and F.
Essential gameplay skills include teamfighting, wave management, map awareness, cooldown tracking, and positioning to maximize impact.

Roles

League of Legends has five core roles on the Summoner's Rift map: Top, Jungle, Mid, ADC (Attack Damage Carry/Bot Laner), and Support, each with unique responsibilities,
influencing gameplay from laning to team fights, from self-sufficient duelists (Top) and objective controllers (Jungle) to map roamers (Mid), late-game damage dealers (ADC), and vision/playmakers (Support).
Mastering a role helps new players learn the game, as they focus on specific champion types and strategic goals.

Tank

Tanks in League of Legends are durable front-line champions designed to absorb damage, initiate fights, and control the battlefield with crowd control (CC).
They protect "squishy" teammates by disrupting enemies, allowing carries to safely deal damage.
Tanks excel at taking focus, peeling for allies, and securing objectives.

Assasin

Assassins in League of Legends are high-mobility, melee damage dealers tasked with infiltrating enemy lines to quickly eliminate high-priority, fragile targets (ADCs, mages) using superior burst damage.
They excel at finding picks, creating 5v4 situations, and roaming to snowball leads, but they are fragile "glass cannons" that require precise timing, positioning, and target selection.

Mage

Mages in League of Legends are primary magic-damage dealers tasked with providing burst damage, zone control, and area-of-effect (AoE) damage, typically from a safe distance in the mid-lane.
They excel at clearing minion waves, scaling with levels to dominate teamfights, and utilizing utility/crowd control to set up kills or peel for teammates.

Support

The Support role in League of Legends acts as the team's primary utility provider, strategist, and protector, mainly operating in the bottom lane to assist the ADC.
Key responsibilities include securing vision control, initiating or peeling in teamfights, and facilitating map pressure, often acting as a "pseudo captain" to coordinate the team.

Marksman

Marksmen (or ADCs) in League of Legends are primary, ranged damage dealers designed to carry teams through high, sustained basic-attack damage (DPS).
Usually played in the bottom lane, their role involves farming for gold to scale into late-game powerhouses, securing objectives like Towers and Dragons, and positioning safely behind teammates to destroy enemy champions.

Fighter

Fighters (or bruisers) in League of Legends are durable, melee combatants that bridge the gap between tanks and damage dealers, excelling in sustained damage (DPS) and extended duels.
They dominate top lane and jungle by creating pressure through split-pushing, disrupting enemy backlines in teamfights, and surviving burst damage.

Gameplay

League of Legends is a 5v5 multiplayer online battle arena (MOBA) where teams compete to destroy the enemy base's core structure, the Nexus.
Players choose from over 160 champions with unique abilities to fight across three lanes (top, mid, bottom) and a jungle, using strategy, teamwork, and item purchases to gain power.

Team Lineup

Top

Winning 1v1 matchups, creating map pressure through split-pushing, and acting as a frontline or disruptor in teamfights.

Jungle

Ganking lanes to create numerical advantages

Mid
